YOUR GROWTH TOOLKIT

Eight ways to help your business bloom.

Eight practical capabilities to help you make clearer decisions. Drag or swipe to explore; hover to pause.

✦AI Business Advisor

Tell us what is slowing your business down. BloomBots breaks the problem into smaller needs and helps you decide what to tackle first.

⌕Smart Tool Discovery

Find software that fits the work you need to do. See what each tool is good at, where it may fall short, and which options deserve a closer look.

⇄Compare & Explore

Look at tools side by side to understand their differences. Compare costs and limitations, then search for cheaper or better alternatives.

$Cost & Value Planner

Enter your budget, team size, and estimated time spent on tasks. Explore what different tools might cost and how much time or money they could save.

◎AI Readiness Check

Answer a few simple questions to see what you should prepare before trying a new tool. Check whether software you already use could help first.

◇Workflow & Tool Preview

See how a task gets done today and how it might work with a new tool. Try sample outputs to get a feel for the idea before making changes.

↗Action Roadmap

Get a step-by-step checklist for trying your chosen solution. Work through setup tasks one at a time and keep track of what comes next.

▤Decision Passport

Keep a record of the tools you considered, your cost assumptions, and your next steps. Save or print your plan so you can refer back to it.
